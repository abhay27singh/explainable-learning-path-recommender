Privacy policy

Last updated 13 September 2026. This is a university research demonstration, not a commercial service.

What this system stores about you

If you create an account, the system stores the username you choose, a password hash produced with scrypt, the role you select, any background details you choose to give, and the record of weeks you mark as studied, passed, failed, or already known. It stores nothing else. There is no email address, no analytics, no advertising identifier, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

What it does not store

Your password is never written to disk in readable form. Nothing you do here is sent to any external service. No data leaves the machine this application runs on.

Security limitations you should know before registering

This deployment is demonstration grade. It has no TLS, so if it is served over a network rather than locally, traffic including your password travels in plain text. It has no rate limiting on sign-in attempts and no account recovery. For these reasons you should choose a password you do not use anywhere else.

Research data

The 25,101 learner records shown to advisers come from the Open University Learning Analytics Dataset, which is published under CC-BY 4.0 and contains no personally identifying information. Those learners are represented by anonymised numeric identifiers assigned by the dataset authors. No attempt is made, and none should be made, to re-identify them.

Deleting your data

Ask the project maintainer to remove your account and study history. Because the store is a single local database file, deletion is immediate and complete.

Automated decisions

Recommendations produced here are advisory. They carry no weight in any assessment, grade, or progression decision, and they are not used for any purpose beyond showing you what the model predicts.

Terms and conditions

Last updated 13 September 2026.

What this is

An academic prototype accompanying the paper Explainable AI-Based Personalized Learning Path Recommendation System for University Students. It demonstrates and reproduces the results reported in that paper. It is not a commercial product.

Use of the system

You may create an account to explore the recommender. Do not attempt to re-identify any learner in the research dataset, do not upload personal data about other people, and do not use the system to make decisions affecting anyone's education or employment.

Accuracy of recommendations

The model predicts the probability of successful completion, which is driven substantially by engagement rather than by understanding. Recommendations are estimates from historical data at one institution and may be wrong for any given learner. They are not academic advice and are no substitute for a human adviser. The Course Finder is rule-based and describes common eligibility patterns that vary by institution.

Availability and warranty

The system is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. It may be taken offline, reset, or changed at any time, and stored accounts and study history may be cleared without notice.

Licensing and attribution

The source code is released publicly for inspection and reproduction. The Open University Learning Analytics Dataset is used under CC-BY 4.0 and is attributed to Kuzilek, Hlosta and Zdrahal (2017). If you build on this work, cite the paper and the dataset.
