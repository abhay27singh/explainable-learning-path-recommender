Privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026. An independent student project, not a commercial service.

Using the site without an account

The Course Finder, Explore, the entrance exam plans and the self-check questions all work without an account. Nothing you choose there is stored.

What an account stores

The username and display name you choose, a password hash produced with scrypt, and your role. For a student: your level of study, your class 12 stream or your course, and anything you choose to add under My details, such as an age band or your highest education.

Then what you do here: the weeks you mark as studied or found hard, the quiz marks you enter, your self-check results, and the courses you save. A short activity log records when you sign up, sign in, fail to sign in, and record or change something, so an admin can answer "I cannot sign in". It never holds a password or your answers to a self-check.

If an adviser looks at your path, the request is logged. Advisers can also write notes about a student; those notes are private to advisers and admins.

There is no email address, no analytics, no advertising identifier and no third-party tracking of any kind.

What it does not store or send

Your password is never written down in readable form. Fonts and every page are served from this site. Nothing you do here is sent to another service. Study links open other sites, such as NCERT, Khan Academy, YouTube, NPTEL, SWAYAM, Skill India and NSDC, which have their own privacy policies.

Security you should know about

A sign-in lasts twelve hours. After eight wrong passwords for one username within fifteen minutes, that username has to wait before trying again. There is no account recovery, and this site has no encryption of its own, so served over a network without HTTPS a password would travel in plain text. Choose a password you do not use anywhere else.

Research data

The 25,101 learner records shown to advisers come from the Open University Learning Analytics Dataset, which is published under CC-BY 4.0 and contains no personally identifying information. Those learners are represented by anonymised numeric identifiers assigned by the dataset authors. No attempt is made, and none should be made, to re-identify them.

Deleting your data

Ask the maintainer, or an admin, to delete your account. Deleting it removes everything listed above that belongs to it, at once: study history, quiz marks, self-check results, saved courses, background details and the activity log.

Automated decisions

Recommendations are advisory. They carry no weight in any assessment, grade, admission or progression decision, and they are not used for anything beyond showing you what to study next.

Terms and conditions

Last updated 6 October 2026.

What this is

A free learning planner for students in India, built as an independent project alongside the paper Explainable AI-Based Personalized Learning Path Recommendation System for University Students, whose results it reproduces on an admin-only page. It is not a commercial product.

Use of the site

You may create an account to plan and track your own learning. Do not attempt to re-identify any learner in the research dataset, do not enter personal data about other people, and do not use the site to make decisions affecting anyone's education or employment.

The weekly path

The model predicts the chance of completing each week successfully, which is driven substantially by engagement rather than by understanding. It learned from historical data at one university and may be wrong for any given student. It is not academic advice and is no substitute for a teacher or adviser.

The Course Finder and entrance exams

The Course Finder is rule-based, not the model. It describes common eligibility patterns, which vary by institution and change from year to year. Exam pages give the published outline of each syllabus and a revision plan; they state no dates, cut-offs or ranks, and the official notice from the conducting body always takes precedence. Self-check questions are written for revision and are not past papers.

Availability and warranty

The site is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. It may be taken offline, reset or changed at any time, and stored accounts and study history may be cleared without notice.
