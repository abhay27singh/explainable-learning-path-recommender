What came from where

and what I left out
  • Three panesEdu.ai. Quick actions get a permanent home instead of hiding inside a card.
  • Week stripEdu.ai's week at a glance, built from activity you actually recorded.
  • Status pillsEvery shot uses them. "Ready · 21 Sep" reads faster than a sentence.
  • One filled tileLearnova. A row of identical tiles gives the eye nowhere to land.
  • Dates on weeksEvery dashboard had a schedule; yours had none. Now the planner sets them.
  • Record of studyTheir certificates, made honest: what you finished, marked as issued by nobody.
  • No ratings or counts"12k enrolled", star ratings: invented numbers, so they are out.
  • No leaderboardsThey lift the top few and discourage the students who need this most.
  • No AI confidence cardWe would have to make the number up. Explanations trace to real values instead.
Mockup, not the live site. Dates, calendar export, dark mode and the record of study are already built and running; this shows how they fit together on one screen.
