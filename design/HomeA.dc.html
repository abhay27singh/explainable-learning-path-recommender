How it works

Three steps. No test to sit, no long setup.

1

Tell us where you are

Class 10, class 12, diploma, undergraduate or postgraduate, and your course if you are on one.

2

Get your next step

On a course you get it week by week from Week 1. At school you get courses that fit your subjects.

3

Mark what you finish

Enter a quiz score or press a button, and the path moves on to the next week.
